What is hosting?

Hosting is your business premises online: the place where your website’s pages, images and videos are kept and sent to every visitor. It can also include email, analytics and databases.

What is a domain name?

Your website’s address, such as logos.co.ug. The domain tells browsers where to look. Hosting is the place they find your site. Search a domain.

How does hosting work?

When someone types your domain, it is turned into the address of a server. That server sends back your website’s files. You rent space on it, by the year.

Which type of hosting do I need?
  • Shared: many sites share one powerful server. Affordable, and the provider handles maintenance and security.
  • Cloud: resources come from several servers, so there is less downtime and room to grow.
  • Dedicated: a whole server for you, with full control.
  • VPS: your own share of a server: dedicated features at a shared price.
