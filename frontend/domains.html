Questions about domains.

Something else on your mind? Call us: we answer 24/7.

What is a domain name?

It is your website’s address on the internet, like logos.co.ug. When someone types it, their browser finds your website. It also gives you email addresses on your own name.

Which ending should I choose?

.co.ug tells customers you trade in Uganda. .com suits a business that sells abroad. .org is the usual ending for NGOs and associations. Many businesses take two, so nobody else can register the other one.

Do I pay every year?

Yes. A domain is registered for a year, then renewed. The first year includes the 10% discount. Renewal prices are on the cards above.

How long does a transfer take?

For .com and .org, usually less than a week: once you approve, the move completes within five days. For .ug and .co.ug, ask us.

Which other endings can I get?

.biz and .info are on our price list too, and we can look up most other endings. Ask sales.
