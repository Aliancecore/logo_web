Five working days, from first call to launch.

What we do each day, and the little we need from you.

  1. Monday

    We talk

    You tell us what you sell and who buys it. We agree on a package and a plan.

    From you: a short call, and your logo if you have one.

  2. Tuesday

    Your name goes live

    We register your domain and set up your business email.

    From you: the name you want, and who needs an address.

  3. Wednesday

    Design

    We design your pages and send you a preview link to comment on.

    From you: your comments on the preview.

  4. Thursday

    Content and SEO

    We write your pages, add your photos and set up basic SEO.

    From you: photos of your work, prices and opening hours.

  5. Live

    Friday

    Launch

    Your site goes live on secure hosting, with SSL. Support carries on 24/7.

    From you: a last look, then the go-ahead.
