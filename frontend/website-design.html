Questions about websites.

The ones business owners ask us most. Something else? Call us: we answer 24/7.

What is a website?

A set of pages under one domain name, like yourbusiness.co.ug, that presents your business online. Think of it as your premises, open to anyone with a phone.

Where is my website stored?

On a hosting server, which is why a website comes with a hosting fee. We look after the server. We still advise you to keep your own copy of your photos and texts.

Does a small business really need a website?

Yes. Customers search online before they buy. A website makes you credible, cuts the cost of printing and adverts, reaches new markets and stays open at night and on Sundays.

Why is my website not bringing in customers?

Usually for one of four reasons:

  • it was built without a clear goal,
  • there is no plan to bring visitors,
  • visitors do not see what is in it for them,
  • nobody tracks who visits.

Our digital marketing team works on all four.
