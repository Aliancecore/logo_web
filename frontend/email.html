Why not keep a free address?

Anyone can open a free address, so customers cannot be sure a message really comes from you. An address on your domain proves it, and our filters keep spam and viruses out.

Yes. Your mailbox works in Outlook, in any web browser and in the mail app on Android and iPhone. We help you set it up.
