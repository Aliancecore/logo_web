Already have a domain? Move it to us.

A transfer keeps your name and adds one year to it. Your domain, website and email then sit with one team.

Some endings, and domains renewed very recently, cannot move straight away. Send us the name and we will tell you.

Ask for a transfer

  1. Unlock the domain

    At the company that holds it today, switch off the transfer lock.

  2. Get the transfer code

    They give you an authorisation code, sometimes called an EPP code. Send it to us.

  3. Approve the move

    An email asks you to confirm the transfer. One click and it starts.

  4. Your domain arrives, a year longer

    We keep it pointed at your website and email, so customers notice nothing.
