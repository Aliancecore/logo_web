What is a logo?

A symbol made of text, shapes and images that identifies your business. It usually has a brand mark (the symbol), a logotype (the name, set in a chosen typeface) and sometimes a tagline.

What is branding?

A brand is how people see your company. It is more than the name or the logo: it is the impression you leave, every time someone sees your sign, your receipts or your page.

Why does branding matter?

It builds trust and credibility, connects you with your customers and makes you stand out from the shop next door.
